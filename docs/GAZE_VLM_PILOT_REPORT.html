Does gaze help a compact VLM understand behavior?

A record of the data, models, experiments, results, compute and remaining gaps in the gaze-conditioned neuro-symbolic study.

Readout

The current pilot shows no convincing improvement from gaze. Seed-0 temporal gaze is essentially tied with video-only for activity and anticipation. Rare-event metrics move only slightly, and task-state performance remains poor. This tests gaze variants in a compact student; it does not test the proposed personalization method; structured transfer was trained but not evaluated.

What remains untested

  • Teacher: SmolVLM2-2.2B-Instruct extraction completed on 33,011 train windows; 14,813 passed schema checks (44.9%). This does not establish factual accuracy.
  • T0/T1/T2 caption and structured transfer students trained across three seeds; development evaluation has not run.
  • Personalization by wearer: not run; wearer identities not verified.
  • Gaze-guided compute allocation: implemented option, not scientifically evaluated.
  • Held-out test, cross-dataset evaluation, mobile energy and latency: not run.

Conclusion: the pilot motivates testing the richer personalized neuro-symbolic framework because simple gaze fusion did not show a clear gain. It provides no evidence yet that structured transfer or personalization works.
